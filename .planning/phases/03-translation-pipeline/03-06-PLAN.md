<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 1: Saeid reviews and corrects the first-pass glossary (D-03)</name>
  <what-built>
    `content/fa-glossary.json` holds a first-pass glossary of 60 to 100 terms, mined from the 26 published English posts. Every term's corpus frequency was counted in code from your own writing, not estimated, and every entry carries a real example sentence from the post it came from. Each entry has a strategy tag: `translate` for a term that gets a Farsi rendering, `transliterate` for one written phonetically in Persian script, and `keep-english` for terms Farsi technical prose normally leaves in Latin script.

    `content/fa-glossary-review.html` is the readable version: a standalone page with one row per term showing Term, Farsi rendering, Strategy, Corpus frequency, Example sentence and a blank Correction column.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    1. Open `content/fa-glossary-review.html` in Chrome.
    2. Read down the Strategy column first. The `keep-english` rows are the ones most likely to be wrong in either direction: a term marked `translate` that Farsi tech writing would never translate, or a term left in Latin script that you would render in Persian.
    3. Then read the Farsi rendering column for the high-frequency terms at the top. Those are the ones that will appear in almost every post, so a wrong rendering there is a wrong rendering everywhere.
    4. Check the example sentences for a handful of entries. They are quoted from your own posts, so they tell you the sense in which each term is actually used.
    5. Send corrections in whatever form suits you: annotate the Correction column and say so, or reply with a list of `term -> new rendering` and `term -> new strategy` lines, or name terms to remove and terms to add. Removing and adding is fine as long as the total stays between 60 and 100.
    6. If a term you expect is missing entirely, name it. It can be added with its real corpus frequency looked up from the candidate artifact.
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Reply "glossary approved" if it stands as is, or give the corrections as a list. Nothing is applied until you answer, and no post is translated with this glossary until it is settled.</resume-signal>
  <files>content/fa-glossary-review.html</files>

Do not edit `content/fa-glossary.json` while this task is open and do not pre-empt his answer by "fixing" entries that look wrong. Transcribe his reply verbatim into the plan summary as the correction list Task 2 works from, so every applied change is traceable to something he actually said.</action>
